Bull vs Bear

Regimes from real BTC daily closes (Yahoo) 2015 → Oct 13 2026. Correlations use weekly returns, so stocks and crypto line up.

Regime rules

🚀 Euphoria: Mayer multiple (price ÷ 200D avg) > 2.0, OR weekly RSI > 80, OR 90-day return in the top 10% (>+83%).
🧊 Capitulation: Mayer < 0.8 OR 90-day return in the bottom 10% (<-28%).
🐂 Bull: above a rising 200D. 🐻 Bear: below a falling 200D. ↔️ Chop: everything else.
Share of days: capitulation 17.2% · bear 14.4% · chop 12.5% · bull 43.7% · euphoria 12.2%. Gold–SPX uses SPX's own regime.

Regime heatmap: correlation (beta)

Cell = weekly correlation; small number = beta (how many % the first asset moves per 1% of the second) · n weeks. Blank = fewer than 8 weeks.
